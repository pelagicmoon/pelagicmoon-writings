When Jean-François Lyotard maps out that "From the read to the seen, we pass from a 'horizontal', flat, atopic negativity, to a 'vertical', deep, place-making negativity" (30) in The Dream-Work Does Not Think, I imagine a category of video essays on YouTube (those by Hoog are good examples) in which two-dimensional visuals begin to blossom with embellishments that look three-dimensional. The two-dimensional 'substratum' is largely monochrome; by contrast, the blooms of 3-D are garishly colorful, signposting the information that we should really pay attention to, whatever the content of that information. Think about the name of a chromosome, which describes nothing about what is in one (the genes, structural elements, etc.) but instead points vaguely to some mysterious object X that is dyed under a microscope.


Earlier, in tracking the path textual fragments take before landing in their places within the text (am I giving it away by using the verb 'land'?), Lyotard answers how "the extract move[s] above the text" but then says that "This movement takes place, therefore, in depth" (7). The choice of 'depth' to describe the distance of the extract carries a different connotation than, let's say, 'altitude', which would be more apt if "above the text" referred to air travel. Depth introduces a below-the-ground feeling which seems to undermine the bird's-eye-view Lyotard momentarily granted his extracts as they each migrated to reassemble their spots. This aboveness, though, a tryst played against the vertigo of the surface below, is a visualization Lyotard cannot yet abandon: the passage "from the energistic to the linguistic," which in his eyes gets our dream to look like it says something to us, is a "selective power directing these upheavals to deposit their products in a readable manner" (30, emphasis added). It may be to further drive a problematic into Lacan's strong theory [Fig. 1] of the signifier [S'] covering the signified [S] from above (which he already does when he writes that in Saussure's original version of the formula, the bar between them is "far from representing repression or censorship" (19) and before any of that puts S' below S) that Lyotard refuses to decide whether the seen falls onto the read from above or carves out a canyon system below the read.
